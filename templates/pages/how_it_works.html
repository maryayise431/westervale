What Happens After You Hold

Once you fund a plan from your balance, the held amount is moved into your trading balance and your plan starts running immediately. Your dashboard and My Holdings page show live performance for every active plan:

  • Progress bar — elapsed vs. total duration, with the current day ("Day X of Y").
  • Current Value — your projected position as it grows linearly toward the maturity payout.
  • Maturity Payout — the full expected return including principal and projected profit.

On the maturity date the return is credited back to your available balance automatically and recorded in your transaction history.
