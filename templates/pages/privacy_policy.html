1. Introduction

Westervale Capital ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have about it when you use our platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Account details such as your name, email address, and password.
  • Identity verification documents as required by local regulation.
  • Payment and deposit information needed to process transactions.
  • Communications you send to our support and advisory teams.

We also collect certain technical data automatically, such as your device type, browser, IP address, and how you interact with the platform.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and provide our services.
  • To process deposits, holdings, withdrawals, and maturity payouts.
  • To verify your identity and comply with legal and regulatory obligations.
  • To communicate with you about your account and improve our platform.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the platform, under strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Regulatory authorities and law enforcement where required by law.
  • Professional advisors where necessary to protect our legal rights.

5. Data Security

We use encryption, access controls, and secure infrastructure to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@westervalecapital.com.

7. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how the platform is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the date above and, where appropriate, by sending you a notice.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at info@westervalecapital.com.
